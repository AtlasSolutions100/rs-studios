Conversion tracking

When someone visits one of your Campaignify pages or sends one of your forms, Campaignify tells your ad accounts, so they can count results and find more people like them. Each event goes from the visitor's browser and from our server with the same id, so it's counted once.

Published pages measuring ad results: {{ pages_on }} of {{ pages_all }}. Only visitors in the US are measured, and never someone who turns on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. You can turn it off per page in the page editor.

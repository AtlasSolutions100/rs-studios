Privacy

Last updated October 2026

The short version

We collect what we need to run your account and make your ads. The only tracking on our own site is the Google Ads tag and, while we advertise there, the Meta, TikTok, Reddit and X pixels, which tell us whether our ads lead to sign-ups and purchases. Where the law asks for consent first, and whenever your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, the Google tag runs without ad cookies and the other pixels don't load at all. Pages, links and forms that our members publish carry no tracking scripts. We do not sell or rent personal information.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your name (if given), email address and a hashed password. Your login uses one cookie that keeps you signed in; it isn't used for anything else.
  • Your brand kit and uploads. Product names, approved claims, audience notes and the product photos you upload.
  • What you make. The briefs you write and the images and videos generated for you, so they're in your library.
  • Purchases. Which credit pack or plan you bought, when, and the amount, plus the Stripe customer and subscription ids that link your plan to your workspace. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • Abuse protection. Your IP address is recorded briefly with sign-up and login attempts to slow down spam and password guessing, and deleted after a day.

Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your own browser, not on our server.

Our own ads, the Google tag and ad pixels

  • The Google Ads tag loads on campaignify.ai and in the app. It tells Google Ads when someone who clicked one of our ads creates an account or buys a plan or credit pack (with the amount and an order reference, never your card or your content). Google may set cookies for this; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Where it runs without ad cookies. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and whenever your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, the tag starts with ad cookies switched off and sends Google only cookieless measurements.
  • Ad pixels. While we advertise on Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, Reddit or X (Twitter), that platform's pixel loads on campaignify.ai and in the app. It records page views, a view of the pricing page, and when someone creates an account or buys a plan or credit pack (with the amount and an order reference). The platforms may set their own cookies for this. Our server may also report the sign-up or purchase to Meta, TikTok and Reddit directly, with your IP address, browser type and the platform's own cookie or ad click id, so a blocked script doesn't lose it. We never send your email, name, card or content to them. See Meta, TikTok, Reddit and X for how they use it.
  • Where the pixels don't load. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (or with a European time zone), whenever your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, on admin pages, and on any page whose address could carry personal details (an invite code, a search). Then they set no cookies and send nothing, and our server doesn't report you either; a small first-party cookie (cfy_ads=0) remembers this.
  • Which ad or link brought you. If you arrive on our site from a link with campaign tags (utm_…) or an ad click id (Google, Meta, TikTok, Reddit or X), we keep those in a first-party cookie for 90 days and save them with your account if you sign up, so we know which of our campaigns work. We don't set this cookie under Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

Pages, links and forms our members publish

Campaignify members can publish pages at campaignify.ai/p/…, share smart links at campaignify.ai/l/… and collect answers with forms. When you visit or use one:

  • Smart links record the time, the website you came from, the kind of device and any UTM tags on the link. We don't store your IP address with the click.
  • Pages count visits. Your IP address is kept for at most a day to avoid counting you twice.
  • Forms store what you type in and send it to the member who owns the form (and to their own tools, if they connected a webhook). That member decides how it's used; ask them, or us, to delete it.
  • Messages from members. If you agree on a member's form to get their emails or texts, we record that you agreed (when, which form, the wording shown, and a one-way hash of your IP address) and send their messages on their behalf. We record whether each email was delivered, opened (approximately) or clicked, so the member's follow-ups can respond. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link; reply STOP to any text to stop texts. Opting out is honoured across all of that member's messages.

Third parties

  • AI generation. To make an ad, your brief and the product photos you pick are sent to the AI generation provider we use. The provider processes them to produce your ad and, under its own terms, may use content to improve its models.
  • Resend delivers emails that members send from their flows, and tells us when they're delivered, bounce or are marked as spam.
  • Twilio delivers text messages that members send from their flows (once a member connects texting), and passes on replies such as STOP and HELP.
  • Stripe processes payments when you buy credits or a plan, and hosts the page where you manage or cancel a plan.
  • Google Fonts. Fonts load from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • Google Ads measures our own ads, as described above.
  • Meta, TikTok, Reddit and X measure our own ads on their platforms through their pixels, as described above.

No other third-party services are used. Everything else is stored on our own server.

Privacy

Last updated October 2026

The short version

We collect what we need to run your account and make your ads, and nothing else. No analytics, no ad pixels, no tracking across sites. We do not sell or rent personal information.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your name (if given), email address and a hashed password. Your login uses one cookie that keeps you signed in; it isn't used for anything else.
  • Your brand kit and uploads. Product names, approved claims, audience notes and the product photos you upload.
  • What you make. The briefs you write and the images and videos generated for you, so they're in your library.
  • Purchases. Which credit pack or plan you bought, when, and the amount, plus the Stripe customer and subscription ids that link your plan to your workspace. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • Abuse protection. Your IP address is recorded briefly with sign-up and login attempts to slow down spam and password guessing, and deleted after a day.

Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your own browser, not on our server.

Pages, links and forms our members publish

Campaignify members can publish pages at campaignify.ai/p/…, share smart links at campaignify.ai/l/… and collect answers with forms. When you visit or use one:

  • Smart links record the time, the website you came from, the kind of device and any UTM tags on the link. We don't store your IP address with the click.
  • Pages count visits. Your IP address is kept for at most a day to avoid counting you twice.
  • Forms store what you type in and send it to the member who owns the form (and to their own tools, if they connected a webhook). That member decides how it's used; ask them, or us, to delete it.
  • Messages from members. If you agree on a member's form to get their emails or texts, we record that you agreed (when, which form, the wording shown, and a one-way hash of your IP address) and send their messages on their behalf. We record whether each email was delivered, opened (approximately) or clicked, so the member's follow-ups can respond. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link; reply STOP to any text to stop texts. Opting out is honoured across all of that member's messages.

Third parties

  • AI generation. To make an ad, your brief and the product photos you pick are sent to the AI generation provider we use. The provider processes them to produce your ad and, under its own terms, may use content to improve its models.
  • Resend delivers emails that members send from their flows, and tells us when they're delivered, bounce or are marked as spam.
  • Twilio delivers text messages that members send from their flows (once a member connects texting), and passes on replies such as STOP and HELP.
  • Stripe processes payments when you buy credits or a plan, and hosts the page where you manage or cancel a plan.
  • Google Fonts. Fonts load from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address as part of that request.

No other third-party services are used. Everything else is stored on our own server.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your account, uploads or ads, email hello@campaignify.ai.

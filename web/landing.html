Built for people who sell

Compliance checks at every step.

If you're an independent rep, your company has rules about what your ads can say, and so does the FTC. Campaignify checks every ad brief, every piece of copy and every landing page before it goes out, and stops the ones that would get you in trouble.

The checks are a guard rail, not legal advice. Always follow your company's policies and the FTC's guidance on endorsements and earnings claims.

  • No income or earnings claimsPromises about pay, income or lifestyle are blocked on every account. Your ads sell the product, not the opportunity.
  • No recruiting in product adsTeam-building and business-opportunity pitches don't belong in a product creative, so they're blocked.
  • No weight-loss or health claims beyond the labelDisease, cure and before-and-after language is blocked. Use the claims printed on your label, nothing more.
  • No discount promisesCoupons, percent-off and free-shipping offers are blocked. Most direct-sales companies set the price, not the rep.
  • Disclosure built inEvery ad comes with the reminder: #ad first in the caption, AI label on. We flag briefs where an AI presenter speaks as a customer.

FAQ

Questions.

Does Campaignify post or run my ads?

No. It plans the campaign, makes the ads, writes the copy and walks you through setting up each ad account, then exports every ad at that platform's sizes. You upload and launch them in the platform's own ads manager, and your ad spend stays there too.

Can I see how my campaign is doing?

For what you run on Campaignify, yes: clicks on your smart links (per day, referrer, country, device and UTM source), views on your pages and submissions to your forms, with each contact's history. Campaignify doesn't read results from the ad platforms; see those in each platform's ads manager.

Which plan do I need?

The free studio makes ads with your starter credits and credit packs. Starter adds the AI campaign planner, copy studio, ad-account setup hub, one landing page and smart links. Growth adds the SEO, AEO & GEO optimizer, the content optimizer, forms and contacts, link analytics and unlimited pages. Scale adds community and courses and the priority queue.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Start with your free credits and buy credit packs when you need more. Plans add monthly credits and the campaign tools; you can cancel a plan anytime and it stays on until the end of the month you've paid for.

Do unused credits roll over?

Plan credits arrive with each monthly payment and expire at your next renewal, and they're used before any other credits. Credits from packs and your free starter credits never expire.

What does a credit buy?

1 credit is $0.01. An image ad is {{ r.image }} credits, a 5-second video {{ r.video5 }}, a 10-second video {{ r.video10 }}, and copying a reference video's motion is {{ r.motion_per_s }} credits per second of the reference (up to {{ r.motion_max }}). The AI writing tools charge what the AI actually used. You always see the price before you generate.

What if a generation fails?

Your credits come back automatically. You only pay for ads that finish.

Does it work for my product?

If you can photograph it, yes. Product hero, Daily routine and your own brief work for anything; a few templates were written for coffee and say so.

Who owns the ads I make?

You can use the ads you make in your own marketing, including paid ads. They're AI-generated, so similar images can come out for other people, and you're responsible for what you post. See the terms.

Does Campaignify guarantee results?

No. Campaignify helps you plan, make and run a campaign. It doesn't promise sales, views or income, and your ads shouldn't either.

Is this from my direct-sales company?

No. Campaignify is an independent product. It isn't a company program and isn't sponsored or endorsed by any direct-sales company. Check your company's policies on advertising and AI content before you post.

Do my ads say they're AI?

Put #ad first in your caption and turn on the platform's AI label. Don't present AI people as real customers or let them describe personal results.

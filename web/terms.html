The short version

Campaignify makes AI-generated ads from your product photos and briefs. You pay in credits, you're responsible for what you post, and you can't use it to make ads that mislead people.

Your account

Use a real email, keep your password to yourself, and one person per account. If your team lead invited you into their workspace, the workspace's credits and ads are shared with that team.

Credits and payments

  • 1 credit = $0.01 of generation. Prices are shown before you generate.

  • Credits have no cash value and can't be transferred or exchanged for money. Purchases are non-refundable except where the law requires otherwise; if something went wrong, email us and we'll make it right.

Your ads

  • You're responsible for checking every ad before you post it, and for following the law, the platform's rules and your company's policies.
  • Disclose ads as ads (for example, #ad first in the caption) and turn on the platform's AI label where it's offered or required. Don't present AI-generated people as real customers or as human-made content.

What you can't make

Our checks block some of this automatically, but the responsibility is yours. Don't use Campaignify for:

  • Income, earnings or lifestyle claims, or recruiting and business-opportunity pitches.

  • Fake reviews or testimonials, or anything that imitates a real person without their permission.
  • Products you don't have the right to sell or advertise, or photos you don't have the right to use.
  • Anything illegal, hateful, sexual, or harmful to minors.

We can refuse a generation or close an account that breaks these rules.

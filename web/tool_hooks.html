How it works

What makes a hook hold

  1. 1

    Start with the viewer

    Open on the problem they have or the moment they are in, not on your brand name.

  2. 2

    Say one thing

    One sentence a person could say out loud. Save the details for the next line.

  3. 3

    Test several

    Run the same ad with three different hooks and keep the one people watch past. The guide to UGC ad hooks has examples by angle.

Hook generator questions

Is the hook generator free?

Yes. {{ per_visitor }} runs a day with no account. Making the ad itself uses credits, and free sign-up includes some.

Do you keep what I type?

No. The brief is sent to the text model to write the hooks and is not saved with anything about you.

Why did it refuse my brief?

Briefs with income, health, discount or recruiting claims are refused, the same as in the ad studio, because ads built on them get rejected.
